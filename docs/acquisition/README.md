# Churnary free-acquisition sprint

Operating window: **August 13–September 11, 2026**
Primary conversion: **confirmed early-access signup**
Budget: **$0**

**Live operating workbook:** [Churnary Acquisition — Pilot Aug 2026](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1kUQE4rLkDkMvtUPckOlmc1KDg_k-oDjOPzh1Kli4HpM/edit)

The workbook is ready to use, but an owner must manually open **Share → General
access** and verify it says **Restricted** before prospect information is added.
The CSV files in this folder remain the portable, version-controlled templates.

This folder is the operating kit for the first acquisition sprint. It optimizes
for qualified signups from independent U.S. repeat-visit businesses, not
unqualified traffic. Nothing in these materials is permission to scrape,
purchase data, automate cold messages, or imply customer results Churnary has
not earned.

## Sprint scorecard

By September 11, aim for:

- 500 qualified website sessions;
- 25 confirmed early-access signups and at least 5% session-to-signup
  conversion;
- 800 unique, manually researched prospects receiving a personalized first
  touch;
- 10 completed pilot-feedback conversations; and
- zero paid ads, paid lead sources, or paid software upgrades.

For this sprint, a **qualified session** is a unique, non-team, non-bot session
that either arrives through the `pilot_aug_2026` campaign or visits a target
vertical/calculator landing page. It is an auditable targeting proxy, not proof
that the visitor owns a business. A **confirmed signup** is a valid signup the
application accepted; repeated submissions by the same email count once.

Choose the week-four vertical using the September 2 scorecard. It is eligible
to scale only if it has generated at least five confirmed signups and at least
5% landing conversion. If no vertical qualifies, do **not** call one a winner:
run a balanced validation cohort of 67 cafés, 67 salons/barbershops, and 66
gyms/fitness studios. Each founder rotates a 4/3/3 daily split so the extra
vertical changes each day.

Regions use the U.S. Census groupings, with D.C. included in the South. A
founder may cross regions only after searching the restricted lead sheet for
the business name, website, and public contact point. Rebalance regions at a
weekly review when a founder cannot find ten qualified prospects; never lower
the qualification standard to fill a quota.

Inbound assignment follows this order:

1. Attribute the signup to the founder named at the start of `utm_content`.
2. Preserve the existing owner on a repeat signup or known tracked relationship.
3. Otherwise, the backend assigns a stable, balanced roster owner from the
   signup email. The assigned founder claims it within five minutes of the
   Discord alert; the period's inbound-duty founder covers absences.

The owner sends a human reply within two business hours. Alerts outside 9:00
a.m.–5:00 p.m. PT are due by 11:00 a.m. PT the next business day. Check the
signup queue at 9:00 a.m., noon, and 3:00 p.m.; Discord is a prompt, not the
system of record.

## Daily operating cadence

All times are local to the founder. Follow-ups do not count toward the ten new
prospects.

| Time | Action | Definition of done |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 9:00–10:00 | Research and qualify 10 new businesses | Required fields and source URL are in the restricted sheet; duplicate and do-not-contact checks pass. |
| 10:00–10:45 | Send touch 1 manually | Each note contains one real, business-specific observation and one permitted tracked link. |
| 10:45–11:15 | Send due touch 2 or 3 messages | One channel only; update sent dates and next action immediately. |
| Noon | Check replies and new signups | Route opt-outs first; claim/respond to inbound within the SLA. |
| 3:00 p.m. | Second inbound/reply check | Book qualified feedback calls; do not pressure a non-response. |
| 4:30 p.m. | Tracker QA | All 10 first touches, replies, dates, and next actions are complete. |

At the weekly review, export only aggregate counts into the scorecard, read the
channel/vertical funnel, inspect five positive and five negative/no-response
examples, and choose **one** copy or placement variable to test next. Do not run
a formal A/B test until each variant can receive at least 200 unique visitors.

September 10 remains an outreach day for the full team, but Aditya owns the
inbound queue that day; September 11 is review/follow-up only.

## Qualification and ethical prospecting rules

A prospect must be a U.S. independent business with 1–5 locations, obvious
repeat-visit economics, an active website or social account, and a public
owner/operator or business-inquiry contact. Use official business websites,
public business social profiles, chambers/directories that allow manual use,
and referrals. Store the source URL so another founder can verify the research.

Non-negotiable rules:

- Research and send manually. No scraping, browser harvesting, purchased lists,
  guessed email patterns, bulk enrichment, or mass DMs.
- Use only a contact point intentionally published for business inquiries. Do
  not store private phone numbers, personal addresses, sensitive traits, or
  unrelated personal details.
- One person, one channel, one three-touch sequence. Never contact the same
  person simultaneously through email, LinkedIn, Instagram, and phone.
- Send on day 0, day 3, and day 7. If there is no reply after touch 3, close the
  sequence for this sprint. Do not manufacture urgency.
- Honor “stop,” “unsubscribe,” or an equivalent request immediately. Set
  `do_not_contact=TRUE`, clear future actions, and never re-add the person.
- Personalization must be relevant and observable: a menu, membership model,
  booking flow, public post, or number of locations. Do not infer revenue,
  churn, demographics, health, or financial condition.
- State that Churnary is early-stage. Label screenshots, calculations, and
  figures as sample/demo data. Never invent a customer, interview, testimonial,
  result, or pilot relationship.
- In communities, answer the question first and follow the community's
  promotion rules. Share a link only when it is allowed and directly useful.
- Never upload the lead sheet to an unapproved tool. Restrict it to the four
  founders, use least-privilege sharing, and remove access when it is no longer
  needed.
